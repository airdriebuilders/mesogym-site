Privacy Policy

Applies to the Meso Gym apps for Android and iPhone and the Meso Gym web app. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

In Short

  • If you're signed in, your training data syncs to your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so it's the same on every device you sign in on. If you're signed out, it stays on your device. More on sync.
  • Progress photos always stay on your device. They are never uploaded. Meso Gym has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no tracking.
  • Accounts are optional in the Android and iPhone apps; you need one to sync, to use the web app or to start or join a Family plan.
  • The only things that leave your device are your account and Family details and your synced training data if you sign in, the training summary you choose to share with a personal trainer (only if you opt in), the one welcome email we send when you create an account, subscription status handled by the stores and RevenueCat, (1) feedback you choose to send, which is emailed to us via FormSubmit, (2) exercise demo videos you choose to play, which load from YouTube, and (3) a standard request for a small update-check file from our website (app.mesogym.com, hosted on Google Firebase Hosting) and the app store.
  • Meso Gym does not read or write Apple Health, Health Connect or any other health data.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your mesocycles, workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, joint-pain ticks), post-session feedback scores, settings, custom exercises, past-meso history, the list of feedback you've sent and onboarding/tip preferences are stored on your device: in the app's private storage on Android or iPhone, or in your browser's local storage on the web. If you're signed out, they stay only on your device and we cannot see, access or recover them. If you're signed in, a copy of your training data also syncs to your account (see Cloud Sync).

On Android, the system's own backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, if you have it turned on) may include the app's data so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings; we don't receive it. On iPhone, the app's data is included in your device's iCloud or computer backups in the same way, managed by Apple under your settings.

You can export your data as a backup file from Settings → Backup (Export JSON); your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. Progress photos are not included in that file. You can delete your training data at any time with Settings → Backup → Erase All Data, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. If you're signed in, Erase All Data also removes it from your account on every device you're signed in on; clearing storage or uninstalling only removes the copy on that device.

Cloud Sync

  • What's stored: when you're signed in, your mesocycles, logged sessions and sets, post-session feedback scores, past-meso history, templates, custom exercises, exercise notes, settings and the list of feedback you've sent (the copy kept in the app, not the email itself) are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, operated by Google), under your account ID. Each item is saved with the time it last changed. Progress photos and the app theme are not synced.
  • Why: so your training is the same on every device you sign in on, including the web app, and so it isn't lost if you change or lose your phone. We use it only to provide sync. We don't analyse it, sell it, share it or use it for advertising.
  • Who can read it: you, when signed in to your account, and a personal trainer only if you choose to share with one (see Sharing With A Trainer). Firestore's security rules stop anyone else, including other members of your family, from reading it, and only you can change it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.
  • How long it's kept: your synced training data is kept until you delete it (a meso, a past mesocycle or Erase All Data) or delete your account.
  • Signed out: nothing is synced. Your training data stays on your device. Signing out leaves the synced copy in your account, ready for when you sign in again.
  • Deleting it: deleting a meso or past mesocycle while signed in removes it from your account on every device you're signed in on. Delete Account removes all your synced training data from the cloud along with your account; workouts already on your phone are kept on that phone until you erase them or uninstall the app.
  • Photos: progress photos are never uploaded, synced or linked to your account.

Progress Photos And The Camera

  • Meso Gym asks for camera access only when you tap Add Photo in Progress Photos, never when you install or open the app. If you'd rather not use the camera, you can choose an existing picture from your photo library instead; on iPhone the system asks for photo library access only if you do that.
  • Photos are compressed and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, never sent to us or anyone else, never linked to your account, and never included in the Meso Gym backup file or in feedback.
  • Delete a photo in Progress Photos, or delete them all by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Erase All Data in Settings removes your training data but not your photos.
  • Like the rest of the app's private storage, photos may be part of your phone's own device backup (Android Auto Backup or iCloud/computer backup) if you have that turned on; that backup is managed by Google or Apple under your settings, and we don't receive it.
  • You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Update Check And Website Files

When the app starts it checks whether an update is required. It downloads a small settings file (app-config.json) from our website app.mesogym.com (Google Firebase Hosting; older app versions read a copy on GitHub Pages) and asks the store for the latest version (the App Store's public lookup on iPhone, Google Play's in-app update service on Android). These are standard requests: nothing about you or your training is added to them. As with any web request, Google, Apple or (for older app versions) GitHub receive technical details such as your IP address and device/browser information and may log them under their own privacy policies; we don't receive or keep those logs. The rest-timer sounds and intro video are part of the app and don't contact any server.

Notifications

Rest-timer notifications are scheduled on your device only. Meso Gym doesn't send push notifications from a server.

Feedback You Send

If you use Feedback (the ? menu at the top of the app → Feedback) and tap send, the following is sent to us by email through the form-to-email service FormSubmit:

AlwaysThe category and your message; the app version; a short device summary (for example "Android 15 · Chrome"), the browser/WebView user-agent string, the screen size, and the time.
Only if you fill them inYour email address (so we can reply); the exercise name and video link you suggest; a suggested exercise's name, muscle group, equipment and example video link.

Nothing is sent unless you tap send. FormSubmit receives the submission in order to forward it to us by email (see FormSubmit's privacy policy), and the email is then stored in our Gmail inbox (Google). We use feedback only to improve Meso Gym and, if you gave an email address, to reply to you. We don't sell it or use it for marketing. Ask us to delete your feedback at any time using our contact form.

The contact form on mesogym.com works the same way: your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message are sent to us through FormSubmit and stored in our Gmail inbox. We use them only to reply to you.

Launch Waitlist

If you signed up through the "Get Notified At Launch" form that was on mesogym.com before launch (our launch waitlist), we store your email address, the platform you picked (if any), the page you signed up on and the time, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We use it only to email you when Meso Gym launches, and nobody else can read it. We delete the list after launch, or sooner if you ask us through the contact form.

Exercise Demo Videos (YouTube)

Demo videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you tap a video. When a video loads, YouTube (Google) receives the usual technical information such as your IP address and device/browser details, under Google's privacy policy. In the Android and iPhone apps, the player is loaded through a small page on our website (app.mesogym.com, hosted on Google Firebase Hosting; older app versions use a copy on GitHub Pages), because YouTube won't play embeds directly from inside an app. That page receives only the video's ID, and Google (or GitHub, for older app versions) may log the request (for example your IP address) under Google's privacy policy or GitHub's privacy statement. "Open in YouTube" opens the YouTube app or website, which is outside Meso Gym.

Intro Video

The intro video is part of the app and plays from your device; it doesn't contact any server.

Children

Meso Gym is a strength-training planner intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Accounts And The Family Plan

If you create an account (needed to sync, to use the web app or to start or join a Family plan), Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, operated by Google) stores: your email address, the name you enter or that Google or Apple provides, the sign-in method, and an account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; Apple then gives us a relay address. For the Family plan we also store which family you belong to, the family's members (name and email, visible to the family organiser), invite codes, and the family subscription's status and renewal date. While you're signed in, your training data also syncs to your account (see Cloud Sync). Progress photos are never uploaded.

Welcome email: when you create an account, we send you one welcome email from Meso Gym's support address, using your account email address and name. We keep a record that it was sent so you only get it once; that record is removed when you delete your account. We don't send marketing emails.

How long it's kept: your account details, Family records and synced data are kept until you delete them or delete your account.

Sharing With A Trainer

Sharing with a personal trainer is opt-in. Nothing is shared unless you join a trainer's group and tap Share With My Trainer. Your trainer then sees a summary on their Trainer Dashboard: your name and when you last trained, sessions done and missed this week, your current meso and week, your main lifts' progress (estimated 1RM) and your last 8 sessions with sets, reps and weights. They see nothing else: no progress photos, feedback or settings. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings, which deletes the shared copy straight away.

You can leave a family, or delete your account, at any time in the Account tab, or without the app via Delete your Meso Gym account. Deleting your account removes your account, family and trainer-sharing records, the welcome-email record and all your synced training data from the cloud. It does not cancel a store subscription; cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings. You can also ask us to delete your account using our contact form.

Subscriptions

Meso Gym Pro (Individual, shown as Single in the apps, £9.99 a month or £59.99 a year, with a 7-day Pro trial for new subscribers; Family £24.99 a month or £149.99 a year, no trial) is bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Meso Gym Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or, if you're signed in, your account ID) and your purchase receipts and subscription status from the store. RevenueCat notifies our server (a Cloudflare Worker) when your subscription starts, renews, is cancelled or ends. If you're signed in, the Worker stores your store subscription's status with your account in Firestore (plan, store, whether it's active and its renewal or end date) so Meso Gym Pro also works when you sign in to the web app or on another device, and, for Family plans, so members' access can be turned on or off. It never receives your card or payment details. Delete Account removes this record. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

On the web you can also subscribe at a lower web price through checkout on our website, which is processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment details; we never see or store your card number. To keep to one 7-day Pro trial per person or payment method, we store Stripe's card fingerprint (a code that identifies a card without revealing the card number) with the accounts and subscriptions that used it; it is kept only for this purpose, never shown to anyone and can't be used to make payments. Stripe tells us your subscription's plan, status and renewal date, which we store with your account (Google Firebase) so Meso Gym Pro unlocks on every device you sign in on. You can manage or cancel a web subscription at any time with Manage Subscription on the website subscribe page. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Your Rights

Your photos never leave your device, and your training data stays on your device unless you sign in, so you control them directly. You can export a copy of your training data from Settings → Backup at any time, delete individual mesos, erase it all with Erase All Data, or remove everything in your account with Delete Account. For your account, synced data and feedback you've sent, you can ask us for a copy, a correction or deletion, and under UK GDPR you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
