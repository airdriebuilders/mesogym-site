About Meso Gym

Short description: Meso Gym is a hypertrophy training app that plans your mesocycles and adjusts every week to how you recover.

Full description: Meso Gym takes the structure serious lifters use, mesocycles, RIR targets, planned weekly volume per muscle and deloads, and does the planning for you. After each session you rate pump, soreness and workload for each muscle, and next week's sets and weights adjust to match. Every one of the 348 exercises has a demo video. Meso Gym works offline, has no ads, and is built by an independent developer in the United Kingdom.

Key Features

  • Mesocycles of 4 to 6 working weeks plus a deload, with each muscle set to Emphasise, Grow or Maintain.
  • RIR targets that drop week by week, suggested weights and a built-in rest timer.
  • Feedback on pump, soreness and workload after each session sets next week's sets and weights.
  • Joint pain flag, exercise swap, and skip or move a session when life gets in the way.
  • 348 exercises, each with a demo video, sorted best first for each muscle.
  • Progress charts for estimated 1RM and weekly sets, a body map and a summary of every block.
  • Family plan for you plus four people, and trainer plans for coaches, studios and gyms.
  • Works offline, with backup export. No ads.
  • Trainer Dashboard for monitoring clients, and Import From RP for RP Hypertrophy app history.
