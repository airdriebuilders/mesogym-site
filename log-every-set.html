RIR Workout Tracker

Log Every Set

Every exercise shows your sets, rep range and RIR target, plus a suggested weight based on last time. Tick a set to log it. Enter the first set's weight and Meso Gym fills it into the other sets for you.

  • A suggested weight from last time
  • A rep range and RIR target on every set
  • Enter one weight and the rest fill in

How It Works

How Logging A Workout Works

  1. 1

    Open Today's Workout

    Every exercise is laid out with its sets, rep range and this week's RIR target, plus a suggested weight from last time.

  2. 2

    Lift And Tick

    Type the weight and reps, or keep the suggestion, and tick the set. Your first weight fills the sets below it.

  3. 3

    Rest, Then Go Again

    Press Start on the rest timer if you want it. It counts down with your screen locked, and you can add or take off 15 seconds.

  4. 4

    Rate It And Save

    Rate pump and workload for each muscle you trained, save the session, and next week is set from how it went.

Know What To Lift

A Workout Tracker That Tells You What To Lift

Most workout logs give you an empty box. Meso Gym gives you a target for every set: the rep range, how close to failure to go, and a weight to start from, so you spend the session lifting rather than guessing.

  • A rep range and RIR target on every exercise, getting harder through the block.
  • A suggested weight from last time, with the increase shown.
  • Short tips explain RIR in plain English the first time you need them.

Fast Logging

Log A Set With One Tap

Type the weight and reps, then tick. The first weight you enter fills the sets below it, and from week two each box shows a grey suggestion based on last time. If you did exactly that, just tick.

  • Change any number if a set goes differently.
  • Myorep, myorep match and drop sets each get their own rows and ticks.
  • A summary after every session with your sets, weights and feedback.

Interval Timer

A Rest Timer Between Sets That Stays Out Of The Way

Each exercise starts with a rest that fits how taxing it is, from 1:00 for small isolation moves to 3:00 for heavy barbell lifts. Change it to 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00 on any exercise.

  • Starts only when you press Start, so it never runs ahead of you.
  • −15s, +15s and Skip sit right above the Finish button.
  • Keeps counting with your screen locked on your phone.
  • Optional: turn the interval timer on or off in Settings.

Mid-Session

Everything Else You Need In The Gym

  • Find Your Starting Weight

    New exercise? A quick warm-up ramp of 12, 8 and 4 reps gets your first working set into the right rep range.

  • Swap An Exercise

    Machine taken? Swap lists alternatives for the same muscle, closest movement first, for today or the rest of the meso.

  • Flag Joint Pain

    Tick Joint Pain and pick a level. Moderate holds the exercise next week, and severe drops a set, lowers the weight and offers a swap.

  • Notes That Follow The Exercise

    Save a cue such as seat height or grip. It shows under the exercise every time it comes up.

  • A Demo For Every Exercise

    All 348 exercises have a short demo video showing proper form, one tap from the workout.

  • History Set By Set

    Open History on any exercise to see every past session of it, set by set.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What Does RIR Mean?

Reps in reserve: how many more reps you could have done before failure. Every exercise in Meso Gym has an RIR target for the week, starting easier and getting closer to failure as the block goes on.

How Does Meso Gym Suggest My Weight?

From week two, each set shows a grey suggestion based on what you lifted last time, with the change shown, such as +2 kg. If you did exactly that, just tick the set.

Do I Have To Type Every Set?

No. Enter the first set's weight and Meso Gym fills it into the sets below it. Change any number if a set goes differently, then tick it.

Can I Set Rest Times Between Sets?

Yes. Each exercise starts with a rest that fits how taxing it is, from 1:00 for small isolation moves to 3:00 for heavy barbell lifts, and you can pick 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00. The timer starts when you press Start, adjusts by 15 seconds and keeps counting with your phone locked. You can turn it off in Settings.

What If The Machine I Need Is Taken?

Tap Swap. It lists alternatives for the same muscle, closest movement first. Swap for today only or for the rest of the meso: your sets carry over and the rep range follows the new exercise.

Can I Try It Before I Pay?

Yes. Try the demo in your browser with no sign-up, or install Meso Gym: you can log your first 2 sessions free, and Individual starts with a 7-day Pro trial.

Does It Work On Iphone, Android And Computers?

Yes. Meso Gym is on the App Store, Google Play and the web at app.mesogym.com. Sign in with your Meso Gym account and your training syncs between them.
