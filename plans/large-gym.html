Questions

Large Gym Questions

What does Large Gym add over Small Gym?

Trainer and client stats, QR check-in at the front desk and client retention reporting, for one location with over 100 members.

How does QR check-in work?

Print the code for the front desk. Members scan it with their phone camera and tap Check In, once per day.

Does check-in open the door?

No. It records a visit for your stats and Needs Attention. Keep your door access system as it is.

What does retention reporting show?

Members still training after 30 days, who trained in the last 14 days, new clients this month and average workouts a week.

Can I see each trainer’s numbers?

Yes. Trainer stats and Revenue And Replies show each trainer’s clients, activity, revenue and reply times.

Is there a trial?

Gym plans start straight away with no trial. Open the demo gym first.
