What Meso Gym Is For

Hypertrophy, Strength And Toning

Meso Gym is built for three goals in the gym. It plans your training in blocks, sets the weight and reps for each set, and adjusts every week to how you recover.

  • Hypertrophy: build muscle
  • Strength: lift heavier
  • Toning: a firmer, more defined shape

The Three Goals

Hypertrophy

Build muscle. Hard sets close to failure in moderate rep ranges, with sets added through the block as you recover.

Good for: bigger arms, chest, back, legs and glutes.

Strength

Lift heavier. Big compound lifts with heavier weights, fewer reps and longer rests, and a clear record of your estimated one-rep max.

Good for: squat, bench, deadlift and overhead press numbers.

Toning

A firmer, more defined shape. The same planned, progressive training as hypertrophy, often with moderate weights and higher reps.

How lean you look also depends on diet, which Meso Gym doesn’t cover.

Meso Gym is a strength training app. It doesn’t plan diets, fat loss, cardio or general wellbeing.

Want A Coach?

Find A Coach in the app lists online coaches who coach through Meso Gym. Filter by what you want help with: Hypertrophy, Strength, Power, Toning or Older Adults, and Beginners, Intermediates or Advanced.

Glossary

The training words you’ll see in Meso Gym, in plain English.

Meso (Mesocycle)
A training block of 4 to 6 working weeks followed by a deload week. Effort and volume build week by week, then the deload lets you recover before the next block.
Deload
An easier week at the end of a meso: fewer sets and lighter weights, well short of failure. Fatigue drops so you start the next block fresh. In Meso Gym the deload is shown as 5 RIR.
RIR (Reps In Reserve)
How many more good reps you could have done when you stopped. 2 RIR means about two reps short of failure; 0 RIR means you could not have done another clean rep. In a 5-week block Meso Gym targets 3, 2, 2, 1 then 0 RIR, then the deload.
Set
One run of reps without stopping, for example 10 reps of bench press. A working set counts towards your volume; warm-up sets don’t.
Rep Range
The number of reps to aim for in each set, for example 8 to 12. When you reach the top of the range at the target RIR, the weight goes up.
Volume
How much work a muscle gets, counted as hard sets per muscle per week. Meso Gym starts each muscle near the low end and adds sets when your feedback says you’re recovering well.
MEV And MRV
Minimum effective volume (the fewest weekly sets that still drive progress) and maximum recoverable volume (the most you can recover from). In the app they show as Start and Max for each muscle.
Progressive Overload
Doing a little more over time: a bit more weight, another rep or another set. It’s the signal that makes muscles and strength keep adapting, and Meso Gym suggests each step for you.
Hypertrophy
Muscle growth. Training for hypertrophy means enough hard sets, close to failure, in moderate rep ranges, with volume rising through the block.
Strength
How much weight you can lift for a few reps. Strength work uses heavier loads, lower reps and longer rests, mostly on big compound lifts like squats, presses and rows.
Power
Strength produced fast: jumping, throwing or moving a weight explosively. Some coaches specialise in it; it’s listed as its own coaching speciality.
Toning
Building a little muscle and keeping body fat low so your shape shows. In the gym it’s the same hard training as hypertrophy, often with moderate weights and higher reps; how lean you look also depends on what you eat, which Meso Gym doesn’t cover.
Pump
The full, tight feeling in a muscle during and just after training. You rate it after each workout and it helps set next week’s sets.
Myorep
A set taken close to failure, then short mini-sets after a few breaths’ rest. It packs a lot of hard reps into little time; Meso Gym counts it as one set for volume.
Drop Set
A set to near failure, then straight away the weight is lowered and you carry on for more reps.
E1RM (Estimated One-Rep Max)
The most you could probably lift once, worked out from a heavier set you did for several reps. It’s how Meso Gym tracks strength without you testing a true max.
