The Training Plan That Adjusts Itself Every Week

Meso Gym builds your mesocycle, fills in every set with a target weight and reps in reserve, then uses how you recovered to set next week's sets and weights. Every change is shown, so you always know why.

  • A full plan from four quick questions
  • Sets, weights and RIR targets pre-filled
  • Deloads built in, history kept for good

How It Works

Plan, Train, Adjust. Repeat Until You've Grown.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Tell Build It For Me how many days you train, your equipment, your experience and your focus. It builds a full mesocycle: 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload.

  2. 2

    Train

    Every set has a target weight, rep range and RIR (reps in reserve). Enter your first weight, the rest fill in, and you tick each set as you go.

  3. 3

    Adjust

    Rate your pump, soreness and workload. Meso Gym sets next week's sets and weights for each muscle and shows you exactly what changed.

Training That Adapts

Next Week Is Built From How This Week Went

No guessing whether to add a set. Meso Gym reads your feedback for each muscle and makes the call, then shows you its reasons.

  1. Before you train a muscle, say how sore it still is from last time.
  2. After the workout, rate the pump and how hard the work felt.
  3. Meso Gym sets next week: it adds a set, holds steady or backs off, and nudges the weights to match your reps.
  4. You see every change before you train, such as +1 Set, +2 kg or Performance Dipped.
  5. When the block ends, a deload week lets you recover so the next one starts fresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mesocycle?

A block of training, usually 4 to 6 weeks, where the work builds week by week and then drops for a deload week so you recover. Meso Gym plans your training in mesocycles: 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload.

What does RIR mean?

Reps in reserve: how many more reps you could have done before failure. Meso Gym gives every workout an RIR target, starting easier and getting closer to failure as the block goes on.

How does Meso Gym decide next week's sets and weights?

From your feedback for each muscle: how sore it was before the workout, and the pump and workload you rated after it. It adds a set, holds or backs off, adjusts the weights to match your reps, and shows you every change with the reason.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Individual starts with a 7-day Pro trial. You can also log your first 2 sessions free without subscribing.

Does it work on iPhone, Android and computers?

Yes. Meso Gym is on the App Store, Google Play and the web at app.mesogym.com. Sign in with your Meso Gym account and your training syncs between them.

Can I import my data from RP Hypertrophy?

Yes. Export your data from the RP Hypertrophy app, then choose the file in Meso Gym under Settings › Backup › Import From RP. Finished mesocycles come across with their logged sets, and unstarted plans become templates. Step-by-step guide.

Can I use kg or lb?

Both. Set your default unit in Settings, and choose kg or lb for each mesocycle when you build it.

I'm a personal trainer. How do my clients join?

Subscribe to a trainer plan on our website, then create an invite code for each client. They sign in on their own phone, enter the code and get full Meso Gym Pro while your plan is active. See trainer plans.
