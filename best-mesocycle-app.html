The Apps In Brief

Each app at a glance, from its own site, and where Meso Gym stands out.

Meso Gym

Best for: RP-style mesocycles at a lower price, families, and trainers and gyms.

Our app. Plans 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload, sets an RIR target on every exercise and adjusts next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload. Family covers up to 5 people. Import From RP.

See Meso Gym Plans

RP Hypertrophy

$34.99 a month or $299.99 a year.

Adjusts from pump, soreness and workload feedback. Meso Gym uses the same kind of feedback for $220 less a year, adds Family and Trainer plans, and has a demo video for all 348 exercises. Import From RP brings your history across.

Meso Gym vs RP Hypertrophy

Mesostrength

Core: $19 a month or $171 a year.Core: monthly or yearly plans.

Adjusts sets and weights from soreness, joint pain, pump and workload. Meso Gym gives you feedback-based mesocycles for less than half the yearly price, with built-in deloads, a Family plan for up to 5 people and a demo video for every exercise.

Meso Gym vs Mesostrength

JEFIT

Workout tracker with an Adaptive Plan on Elite.

Elite's Adaptive Plan runs 4-week blocks with a deload, adjusted from your logged performance. Meso Gym sets next week's volume from your pump, soreness and workload, puts an RIR target on every exercise, lets you run 4, 5 or 6 working weeks, and adds Family and Trainer plans.

Meso Gym vs JEFIT

Alpha Progression

Pro: $79.99 a year.

Pro adds a plan generator, weight and rep recommendations, RIR, periodisation and deloads. For the same yearly price, Meso Gym builds every block as a mesocycle with a deload, sets next week's volume from your pump, soreness and workload, and adds Family and Trainer plans.

FAQ

What is a mesocycle app?

An app that plans your training in blocks of several weeks, usually adding volume or load week by week and ending with a deload, instead of just logging sets.

What is the best mesocycle app?

For RP-style mesocycles where your pump, soreness and workload feedback set next week's volume, Meso Gym is our pick. It costs the least of the feedback-based apps here at $79.99 a year, it's the only one with a Family plan listed, and it works out the starting weights for your next meso from your whole last meso.

What is the cheapest feedback-based mesocycle app?

Of the apps here that set your volume from pump, soreness and workload feedback, Meso Gym is the cheapest at $79.99 a year, against $171 for Mesostrength Core and, against Mesostrength Core and $299.99 for RP Hypertrophy. Meso Gym Family covers up to 5 people for $199.99 a year (last checked 26 October 2026).

Which mesocycle apps have a family plan?

Meso Gym Family covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year. We couldn't find a family plan listed for the other apps here.

Which mesocycle apps work for trainers?

Meso Gym has Trainer plans (PT Starter, Pro Trainer, Studio and Gym) with a Trainer Dashboard in the Android app and the web app (not the iPhone app); see Trainer Plans. JEFIT's site says Coach Mode is temporarily unavailable, and we couldn't find client monitoring for RP Hypertrophy or Alpha Progression.

Can I move my RP Hypertrophy history to Meso Gym?

Yes. Import From RP brings across your finished mesocycles, logged sets and unstarted plans. See Switch From RP.
