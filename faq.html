FAQ

What is a mesocycle?

A training block of 4 to 6 working weeks followed by a deload week. Effort and volume build through the block. See the Training Guide.

What does RIR mean?

Reps in reserve: how many more good reps you could have done. 2 RIR means you stopped about two reps short of failure.

What are MEV and MRV?

MEV is the minimum effective volume, the fewest sets that still drive progress. MRV is the maximum recoverable volume, the most you can recover from. Meso Gym ramps sets from near MEV toward MRV.

How many sets does week 1 start with?

Each muscle starts near the low end of what it needs to grow, a little higher for Advanced lifters. Every exercise gets some sets, the most taxing ones like heavy barbell lifts start with fewer, and lighter exercises take the rest. New lifters also start with shorter sessions. After that, your feedback adds or removes sets week by week.

Why do I need a deload?

It lets built-up fatigue drop so you can start the next block fresh. See Deload Weeks.

Can I use pounds instead of kilos?

Yes. You can choose kg or lb, and you can set a default unit in Settings. Our screenshots show kg.

Where can I use Meso Gym?

On iPhone from the App Store, on Android from Google Play, and in the Web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.

Is there a trial?

Yes, Pro starts with a 7-day Pro trial. Family has no trial.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play or the App Store, so cancel there. Restore purchases from inside the app.

Where is my data stored?

Your training is saved on your phone. Sign in and it also syncs to your Meso Gym account, so it's the same on your phone and in the web app, and it still works offline. Progress photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
