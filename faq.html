FAQ

What is a mesocycle?

A training block of 4 to 6 working weeks followed by a deload week. Effort and volume build through the block. See the Training Guide.

What does RIR mean?

Reps in reserve: how many more good reps you could have done. 2 RIR means you stopped about two reps short of failure.

What are MEV and MRV?

They're the low and high ends of how many sets a muscle needs. MEV (minimum effective volume) is the fewest sets that still drive progress; MRV (maximum recoverable volume) is the most you can recover from. In the app they're shown as Start and Max for each muscle, and your sets build from near Start towards Max.

Can Meso Gym build my plan for me?

Yes. Build It For Me asks four questions (how many days a week you can train, what equipment you have, how long you've been lifting and which muscles to focus on) and plans the whole mesocycle. You can also start from one of 11 starter splits or build your own. See Build Your Mesocycle.

How many sets does week 1 start with?

Each muscle starts near the low end of what it needs to grow, a little higher for Advanced lifters. Every exercise gets some sets, the most taxing ones like heavy barbell lifts start with fewer, and lighter exercises take the rest. Beginners also start with shorter sessions. After that, your feedback adds or removes sets week by week.

Why do I need a deload?

It lets built-up fatigue drop so you can start the next block fresh. See Deload Weeks.

Can I use pounds instead of kilos?

Yes. Choose kg or lb when you create a mesocycle; each mesocycle keeps its own unit. Set the unit new mesocycles start with in Settings → Default Unit. Our screenshots show kg.

Can I bring my training from the RP Hypertrophy app?

Yes. Export your data from RP, then use Import From RP in Meso Gym. Your mesocycles, sets and ratings come across. It's in the web app now and in the iPhone and Android apps from version 1.4.0. See Switch From RP.

Where can I use Meso Gym?

On iPhone from the App Store, on Android from Google Play, and in the web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. You can log your first 2 sessions free, with no card. After that, the Individual plan starts with a 7-day Pro trial.

Is there a trial?

Yes, the Individual plan starts with a 7-day Pro trial, in the app or on the website (one trial per account). Family and trainer plans have no trial.

Can I subscribe on the website instead?

Yes. Subscribing on the website costs at least 10% less than in the app. Sign in with the same Meso Gym account and the app unlocks Pro. See Website Prices.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions bought in the app are managed through Google Play or the App Store, so cancel there, and tap Restore Purchases in the app to restore one. Website plans are managed with Manage Subscription on app.mesogym.com/subscribe.

I'm a personal trainer's client. Do I need to pay?

No. Enter the invite code from your trainer in the Account tab and you get full Meso Gym Pro while their plan is active. See Trainer Plans.

Where is my data stored?

Your training is saved on your phone. Sign in and it also syncs to your Meso Gym account, so it's the same on your phone and in the web app, and it still works offline. Progress photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account tab → Delete Account. Without the app, see Delete Your Account.
