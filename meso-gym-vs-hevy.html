Which Should You Pick?

Pick Hevy if you want a cheap or free tracker, already follow your own program, and like a social feed or logging from your watch. Its free version is generous and Pro is $23.99 a year in the US.

Pick Meso Gym if you want the app to plan the training for you: mesocycles of 4 to 6 weeks with a deload, an RIR target and suggested weight on every exercise, and sets that go up or down next week from your pump, soreness and workload ratings. A Family plan covers up to 5 people.

For Trainers And Gyms: Meso Gym Trainer plans give every client Pro on their own phone, and you follow their training on the Trainer Dashboard. See Trainer Plans

FAQ

Is Meso Gym better than Hevy?

They do different jobs. Hevy is a workout tracker with a free version and a social feed. Meso Gym is a hypertrophy planner that builds mesocycles with deloads and adjusts your volume each week from your feedback. If you want the plan done for you, try Meso Gym; if you want a cheap tracker for your own program, Hevy is a strong choice.

How much is Hevy Pro compared with Meso Gym Pro?

In the US, Hevy Pro is $2.99 a month, $23.99 a year or $74.99 for lifetime. Meso Gym Pro is $13.99 a month or $79.99 a year, with a 7-day Pro trial. Prices in other countries may differ.

Does Hevy plan mesocycles and deloads?

We couldn't find mesocycles or planned deloads listed on Hevy's website or store listing (last checked Oct 2026). Hevy Pro includes Hevy Trainer, which builds a program and guides progressive overload. Meso Gym plans every block with a deload built in.

Can I try Meso Gym free?

Yes. Pro starts with a 7-day Pro trial, so you can run your first week before you pay. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play, the App Store or online.

Is there a Meso Gym web app?

Yes. Alongside the iPhone and Android apps, there's a Web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.

Can personal trainers and gyms monitor clients in Meso Gym?

Meso Gym Trainer plans (Starter, Pro Trainer, Studio and Gym) give each client full Meso Gym Pro on their own phone; clients join with an invite code. You can monitor your clients' logged workouts and progress on the Trainer Dashboard on Android and the Web app. Hevy offers coaching through Hevy Coach, a separate product on hevycoach.com.
