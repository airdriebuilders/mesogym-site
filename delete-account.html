Delete Your Meso Gym Account

For the Meso Gym apps for Android and iPhone (developer: Meso Gym). Last updated: 25 October 2026.

In The App (Fastest)

  1. Open Meso Gym and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm. If asked, sign in again first.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without The App

Use our contact form, write Delete my Meso Gym account in the message, and give the email address you signed in with (for Sign in with Apple, the address Apple shared with us, which may be a private relay address). We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What Is Deleted

  • Your Meso Gym account: email address, name and sign-in method (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your family records (Cloud Firestore): your membership, any invite codes you created, and the family itself if you organise it. Members of a family you organise lose Pro through that family.
  • Your synced training data (Cloud Firestore): the mesocycles, logged sessions, past mesocycles, templates, custom exercises, exercise notes and settings synced to your account.
  • The record of your App Store or Google Play subscription status kept with your account.

What Is Kept, And For How Long

  • Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Feedback or emails you sent us are handled as described in the privacy policy; you can ask us to delete those too.
  • The copy of your workouts, plans and history on your phone is kept on that phone (progress photos were never uploaded) until you delete the app or its data. Export a backup first if you want to keep your training history somewhere else (Settings → Backup).
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions), or cancel a website subscription (Manage Subscription on app.mesogym.com/subscribe) before you delete your account. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Questions: use our contact form. See also our privacy policy.
