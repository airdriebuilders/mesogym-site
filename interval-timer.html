Interval Timer

Each exercise starts with a rest that fits how taxing it is, from 1:00 for small isolation moves to 3:00 for heavy barbell lifts, and you can change it to 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00. The timer starts only when you press Start, adjusts by 15 seconds, and keeps counting with your screen locked.
