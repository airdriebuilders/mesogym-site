Progressive Overload App

Training That Adapts

After each workout, rate pump, soreness and workload for every muscle you trained. Meso Gym adds or removes sets for that day next week, and shows you the change before you save.

  • Rate pump, soreness and workload in seconds
  • Sets and weights adjust for every muscle
  • Every change shown before you train

How It Works

Next Week Is Built From How This Week Went

  1. 1

    Before You Train

    Say how sore each muscle still is from last time.

  2. 2

    After The Workout

    Rate the pump and how hard the work felt for every muscle you trained. Each answer has a one-line description.

  3. 3

    Meso Gym Plans Next Week

    It adds a set, holds steady or backs off, and nudges the weights to match your reps.

  4. 4

    You See Every Change

    Session Saved shows this week against next week, such as +1 Set, +2 kg or Performance Dipped.

Quick Feedback

Rate Pump, Soreness And Workload In Seconds

After each workout, rate the pump and workload for every muscle you trained. Before the next one, it asks how sore you got. No spreadsheets and no guesswork about whether you've done enough.

  • Per muscle, not per workout, so a sore back doesn't hold your arms back.
  • A one-line description for every answer.
  • See the change straight away, such as an Up One Set badge.

No Surprises

See Next Week Before It Happens

Save the session and Meso Gym compares this week with next week for every exercise. Green pills show an added set or a heavier weight. When something holds, a short reason tells you why.

  • Weights go up in small steps that suit the equipment.
  • Each muscle gains sets gradually, only when your feedback says it's recovering.
  • Still sore or overdoing it? Sets come down and the weight holds.

Through The Block

Effort Builds Week By Week, Then You Deload

Each week the RIR target drops, so you train a little closer to failure: Build, Push, Peak, then a lighter deload week to recover and start the next meso fresh. That's progressive overload planned for you, not left to memory.

  • 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload.
  • Joint pain adjusts one exercise, not the whole plan.
  • Your next meso starts from your last one, with starting weights worked out from the whole block.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is progressive overload?

Gradually asking more of a muscle over time, with more weight, more reps or more sets, so it keeps a reason to grow. Meso Gym plans it for you, week by week, from your own feedback.

How does Meso Gym decide next week's sets and weights?

From your feedback for each muscle: how sore it was before the workout, and the pump and workload you rated after it. It adds a set, holds or backs off, adjusts the weights to match your reps, and shows you every change with the reason.

What if I'm still sore?

Sets come down and the weight holds, so the muscle gets time to recover before you push it again.

Will I see the changes before I train?

Yes. When you save a session, Session Saved compares this week with next week for every exercise. Green pills show an added set or a heavier weight, and when something holds, a short reason tells you why, such as Performance Dipped.

Why is there a deload week?

Each week the RIR target drops so you train a little closer to failure. After the hardest week, a lighter deload week lets built-up fatigue drop so the next block starts fresh.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Try the demo in your browser with no sign-up, or install Meso Gym: you can log your first 2 sessions free, and Individual starts with a 7-day Pro trial.
