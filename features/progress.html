Progress Tracking

See at a glance which muscles are getting enough work, how far through your meso you are and whether you're getting stronger.

A Body Map Of Your Training

Front and back figures are coloured by each muscle's average weekly sets: Not Trained, Under-Trained, On Track or Over-Trained. Tap a muscle to see everything you've logged for it.

Power Level And Weekly Progression

Power Level fills as you complete your meso's planned sets, reaching 100% at your peak week. Weekly Progression shows each week's target, so you always know what finishing the week will take.

Strength Per Exercise

Pick an exercise to plot your estimated one-rep max and top-set weight over time. Myorep sets are left out and drop sets count by their top part, so the trend reflects real straight sets.

Weekly Sets Per Muscle

Pick a meso and a muscle to compare planned and completed sets for every week, deload included.

Progress Photos That Stay Private

Take a photo every two weeks and compare Start vs Latest on a timeline. Photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Related Features

  • Plan And History: Your whole meso, past sessions and every workout summary.
  • Training That Progresses Itself: Your feedback sets next week's sets and weights.
  • Myorep Sets: Mini-sets after a near-failure set, with the target reps shown.
