Family Plan

One subscription for up to 5 people.

Currency:

How It Works

  1. The plan owner subscribes to Family ($33.99/month or $199.99/year).
  2. The owner invites up to 4 people with a code, so 5 people in total.
  3. Each person uses Meso Gym on their own phone, with their own training.

Good To Know

  • Family needs a Meso Gym account. The single Pro plan doesn't.
  • Family has no trial. Try Meso Gym first with the 7-day Pro trial.
  • Prices match the app store for the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. In other countries you'll see the local store price at checkout.
