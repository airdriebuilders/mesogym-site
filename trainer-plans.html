Trainer Plans

Meso Gym Pro for personal trainers, studios and gyms, and every client you coach.

Currency:

Who It's For

  • Personal trainers who want every client on a proper hypertrophy plan.
  • Studios with a team of coaches and a regular client list.
  • Gyms that want to offer Meso Gym Pro as a member extra.

How It Works

  1. You subscribe to a trainer plan on our website and sign in to the app with the same Meso Gym account.
  2. You create an invite code for each client from the Account tab.
  3. Each client signs in on their own phone, enters the code and gets full Meso Gym Pro: mesocycles, auto-progression, full history and progress charts.
  4. Remove a client any time and their place is free for someone new.

Trainer Dashboard

See your clients' logged workouts, lift progress and missed sessions in one place. Clients choose whether to share their training with you. The Trainer Dashboard is in the Android app and the web app (not the iPhone app).

Questions

Is there a trial?

Trainer plans don't include a trial. You can try Meso Gym yourself first with the 7-day Pro trial, then move to a trainer plan.

How do clients join?

From the Account tab you create an invite code for each client. They sign in to Meso Gym with their own account, enter the code, and get Meso Gym Pro straight away. Codes work once and expire after 7 days.

Do clients need to pay?

No. Clients get full Meso Gym Pro free while your plan is active.

Can I remove a client?

Yes. Remove them from the Account tab and their place is free for someone else. A client can also leave at any time.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage Subscription on app.mesogym.com/subscribe. You can cancel any time and you keep the plan, and your clients keep Pro, until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens when my plan ends?

Your clients lose Pro. Their own training and history stay in the app and in their own account. If you subscribe again, the same clients get Pro back.

Can I change tier?

Each tier is a separate plan. If you need more client places, subscribe to a bigger tier and cancel the old one, or get in touch through the Contact page and we'll help.

Which currencies can I pay in?

GBP, USD, CAD, AUD and NZD. Choose your currency above and checkout charges in that currency.
