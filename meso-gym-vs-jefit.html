Meso Gym vs JEFIT

JEFIT is a workout tracker with an Adaptive Plan on Elite. Meso Gym is built around feedback-based mesocycles that adjust from how you recovered. Here's how they compare.

The Short Answer

Meso Gym is built for RP-style mesocycles: your pump, soreness and workload feedback set next week's volume, every exercise has an RIR target, and you choose 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload. It adds a Family plan for up to 5 people and Trainer plans with a Trainer Dashboard.

Why Pick Meso Gym?

Pick Meso Gym if you want RP-style training: volume set from your pump, soreness and workload feedback, an RIR target on every exercise, plus a Family plan for up to 5 people.

For Trainers And Gyms: Meso Gym Trainer plans give every client Pro on their own phone, and you follow their training on the Trainer Dashboard. See Trainer Plans

FAQ

How much is Meso Gym Pro?

Meso Gym Pro is $13.99 a month or $79.99 a year and starts with a 7-day Pro trial, so you can run your first week before you pay. Family covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year.

How are Meso Gym's mesocycles different from JEFIT's?

Meso Gym plans 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload, sets next week's volume from your pump, soreness and workload feedback, and gives every exercise an RIR target. JEFIT's Adaptive Plan (Elite) runs 4-week blocks with a deload and updates from your logged performance; we couldn't find pump or soreness ratings on JEFIT's site (last checked 26 October 2026).

Does JEFIT have a family plan?

We couldn't find one on JEFIT's website (last checked 26 October 2026). Meso Gym Family covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year.

Can trainers monitor clients in JEFIT?

JEFIT's site says Coach Mode is temporarily unavailable (last checked 26 October 2026). Meso Gym Trainer plans let you follow every client on the Trainer Dashboard in the Android app and the web app (not the iPhone app). See Trainer Plans.

Is there a Meso Gym web app?

Yes. Alongside the iPhone and Android apps, there's a web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.
