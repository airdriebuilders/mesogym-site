Learn As You Go

A short intro video and in-the-moment tips get you started, and the ? at the top right of every screen opens a menu with How To Use Meso Gym, the built-in step-by-step guide, plus All Features and Feedback. Your training is saved on your phone and, when you sign in, syncs to your Meso Gym account and the web app. It still works offline, and backup export is in Settings.
