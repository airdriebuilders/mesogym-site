Gym Member App

Why Gyms Add Meso Gym

A gym member app that gives people a reason to stay. Members who train without a plan stall, and members who stall stop coming. Meso Gym gives every one of them a plan, under your name.

  • A Real Program For Every Member

    Not just the members who pay for personal training. Everyone gets a proper mesocycle for Hypertrophy, Strength or Toning that adjusts every week from what they log.

  • A Perk That Keeps Members

    Meso Gym Pro is included for every member on your plan, so it’s a premium extra you can put in your membership. Members who can see their lifts going up have a reason to keep coming back.

  • Your Coaches In One Place

    Each coach sees their own clients, their logged sessions and who needs attention, and messages them inside the app.

  • Your Gym’s Brand

    Your logo and colours in every member’s app, and on the emails your coaches send.

    Keep the booking, membership and class app you already use. Meso Gym adds the training side, so there’s nothing to move over.

  • Live The Same Day

    Check out on the website, add your logo, invite your coaches and members. There’s no separate app to publish or wait for.

Gym Programming Software

Gym Programming Software That Does The Planning

Meso Gym gives each member a structured training program that adjusts itself, the same way a good coach would, so your team spends its time coaching instead of writing spreadsheets.

  • Mesocycles, not random workouts. Each block builds training volume step by step and ends with a deload.
  • Progressive overload, done for them. Members log each set, and next week’s weights, reps and sets adjust from how it went.
  • Build It For Me. Four questions and the whole block is planned. Coaches can use templates or build their own.
  • Every set logged. Full history, progress charts and 348 exercise demo videos, on iPhone, Android and the web.
  • Three clear goals. Hypertrophy, Strength and Toning, so every program has a clear purpose.

For Your Coaches

Personal Training Software For Your Whole Team

Each trainer has their own login and sees only their own clients. You see everyone.

  • Coach-to-client messaging. Text, photos and replies to check-ins and form checks inside the app. As the owner, you can read the messages your trainers send.

  • Stats, QR check-in and retention reporting. Trainer and client stats, front-desk check-in by QR code, and reporting on who is staying (Large Gym and up).

    Gym Owner FAQ

    Questions From Gym Owners

    Is Meso Gym Management Software?

    No, and that’s on purpose. Meso Gym doesn’t do memberships, billing, class timetables or door access. It works alongside the gym management software and class booking app you already use and adds what they don’t do: proper training programs for every member.

    Do Members Pay Anything?

    No. Every member or client on your plan gets full Meso Gym Pro on their own phone or the web, for as long as your plan is active.

    Is It A White Label App?

    Your gym’s logo and colours appear in every member’s app and on your coaches’ emails. The app itself is Meso Gym on the App Store and Google Play, so there’s no separate app to publish or wait for.

    Can Members Train Without A Coach?

    Yes. Build It For Me plans a whole block from four questions, and every program adjusts each week from what the member logs. Members who want more can be coached by one of your trainers.

    Which Training Goals Does It Cover?

    Hypertrophy, Strength and Toning. It’s built for lifting in the gym.

    Is There A Free Trial?

    Gym plans start straight away with no trial. Try The Demo Gym first: it opens a sample gym with 4 trainers and 16 clients, takes an hour and saves nothing.

    Which Devices Does It Work On?

    Members use iPhone, Android or the web. Coaches and owners manage clients in the Android app or the web app.

    We Have Several Gyms. Which Plan Do We Need?

    Corporate: £350 a month or £2,100 a year for the first location, plus £175 a month or £1,050 a year for each extra location. Each location is verified and paid into its own Stripe account. Send us the Ask About Corporate form on this page and we’ll set it up with you.

    Can I Move Members Between Coaches?

    Yes. Move a member to another coach and their history, chat and check-ins move with them.

    Which Currency Can I Pay In?

    GBP, USD, CAD, AUD or NZD. Choose one above and checkout charges in that currency.
