Meso Gym vs Mesostrength

Both apps plan mesocycles and adjust your training from how you recovered. Here's how they compare on price, plans and features, using each app's own website.

The Short Answer

Both are feedback-based mesocycle apps. Meso Gym costs less than half as much ($79.99 a year vs $171 for Mesostrength Core in the US). It also has a Family plan for up to 5 people, built-in deloads, starting weights worked out from your whole last meso and a demo video for every exercise.

FAQ

Is Meso Gym cheaper than Mesostrength?

Yes. In the US, Meso Gym Pro is $79.99 a year or $13.99 a month. Mesostrength lists Core at $171 a year or $19 a month, and Pro at $259 a year or $29 a month (last checked 26 October 2026).

Can I try Meso Gym before I pay?

Yes. Pro starts with a 7-day Pro trial, so you can run your first week before you pay.

Does Mesostrength have a family plan?

We couldn't find one on Mesostrength's website (last checked 26 October 2026). Meso Gym Family covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year in the US.

Does Meso Gym have built-in deloads?

Yes. Every mesocycle runs 4, 5 or 6 working weeks, then a deload, and next week's sets are set from your pump, soreness and workload feedback.

Can personal trainers and gyms use Meso Gym?

Yes. Meso Gym Trainer plans (Starter, Pro Trainer, Studio and Gym) give each client full Meso Gym Pro, and you follow their training on the Trainer Dashboard on Android and the Web app. See Trainer Plans.

Can I import my RP Hypertrophy data into Meso Gym?

Yes. Import From RP brings across your finished mesocycles, logged sets and unstarted plans. See Switch From RP.
