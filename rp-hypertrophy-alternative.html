An RP Hypertrophy Alternative For Less

Meso Gym vs RP Hypertrophy: mesocycles, RIR targets and feedback-based volume, side by side.

RP Hypertrophy is a well-known training app built around mesocycles, RIR and weekly feedback. Meso Gym trains the same way: you plan a block, log every set against an RIR target, rate how each muscle felt, and next week's sets adjust. Here's how the two compare.

Switching From RP?

  • The ideas carry over: mesocycles, RIR targets, deload weeks and pump, soreness and workload feedback.
  • Build a block in a few taps: pick 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload, your experience level and days per week, then choose a template or your own exercises.
  • Try it free for 7 days before you pay. See Getting Started and Pricing.

FAQ

Is Meso Gym a good RP Hypertrophy alternative?

If you like RP-style training, yes. Meso Gym plans mesocycles with RIR targets and a deload week, and adjusts next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload ratings. Pro is $13.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, against $34.99 a month or $299.99 a year for RP Hypertrophy.

Does Meso Gym have a free trial?

Yes. Pro and Family both start with a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play or the App Store.

Can I build my own program in Meso Gym?

Yes. Start from a ready-made template or build your own routine, choose 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload and your days per week, and set each muscle to Emphasise, Grow or Maintain.

Is Meso Gym on iPhone?

Meso Gym is on Google Play now. The App Store version is coming soon; join the waitlist on the Download page and we'll tell you the day it's live.

Is Meso Gym affiliated with RP Strength?

No. Meso Gym is an independent app. RP Hypertrophy is a trademark of its owner, and this comparison uses information published by RP and independent reviews, checked in October 2026.
