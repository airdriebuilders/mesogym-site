Switching From RP?

Mesocycles, RIR and muscle priorities work the way you're used to, so most people are set up in a few minutes. You can also import your RP Hypertrophy training history.

  1. Install Meso Gym And Start Your 7-Day Pro TrialRun your first week on Pro before you pay.
  2. Build Your BlockLet Build It For Me plan it from four questions, or pick 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload, your experience level and days per week, then choose a template or your own exercises.
  3. Set Your PrioritiesEmphasise, Grow or Maintain each muscle.
  4. Train And Give FeedbackLog every set against its RIR target, rate pump, soreness and workload, and next week adjusts.

FAQ

Is Meso Gym a good RP Hypertrophy alternative?

Yes, if you like mesocycle training. Meso Gym plans blocks with RIR targets and a deload week, and adjusts next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload ratings. Pro is $79.99 a year, $220 less than RP Hypertrophy's $299.99.

Can I try Meso Gym before I pay?

Yes. Pro starts with a 7-day Pro trial, so you can run your first week before you're charged. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play, the App Store or, for website plans, Manage Subscription on app.mesogym.com.

Can I train with my partner or family on one subscription?

Yes. The Family plan covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year. Each person trains on their own phone or the web app, with their own programme.

Can I build my own programme in Meso Gym?

Yes. Let Build It For Me plan a block from four questions, start from one of 11 starter splits or build your own routine, choose 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload and your days per week, and set each muscle to Emphasise, Grow or Maintain.

Is there a Meso Gym web app?

Yes. Alongside the iPhone and Android apps, there's a web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.

Can personal trainers and gyms monitor clients in Meso Gym?

Meso Gym Trainer plans (PT Starter, Pro Trainer, Studio and Gym) give each client full Meso Gym Pro on their own phone; clients join with an invite code. You can monitor your clients' logged workouts and progress on the Trainer Dashboard in the Android app and the web app (not the iPhone app). We couldn't find client monitoring listed in RP's app information (last checked 26 October 2026). See Trainer Plans.

Is Meso Gym affiliated with RP Strength?

No. Meso Gym is an independent app. RP Hypertrophy is a trademark of its owner, and this comparison uses information published by RP, last checked 26 October 2026.
