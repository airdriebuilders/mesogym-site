FAQ

Is Meso Gym a good RP Hypertrophy alternative?

Yes, if you like mesocycle training. Meso Gym plans blocks with RIR targets and a deload week, and adjusts next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload ratings. Pro is $79.99 a year in the US, $220 less than RP Hypertrophy's $299.99.

Can I try Meso Gym before I pay?

Yes. Pro and Family both start with a 7-day free trial, so you can run your first week before you're charged. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play or the App Store.

Can I train with my partner or family on one subscription?

Yes. The Family plan covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year. Each person trains on their own phone with their own program.

Can I build my own program in Meso Gym?

Yes. Start from a ready-made template or build your own routine, choose 4, 5 or 6 working weeks plus a deload and your days per week, and set each muscle to Emphasise, Grow or Maintain.

Is Meso Gym on iPhone?

Meso Gym is on Google Play now. The App Store version is coming soon; join the waitlist on the Download page and we'll tell you the day it's live.

Is Meso Gym affiliated with RP Strength?

No. Meso Gym is an independent app. RP Hypertrophy is a trademark of its owner, and this comparison uses information published by RP, checked in October 2026.
