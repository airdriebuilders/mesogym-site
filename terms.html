Terms Of Use

Meso Gym · Draft, October 2026

The app

Meso Gym helps you plan and log hypertrophy training. It is not medical advice; train within your limits. Its default volume landmarks are based on published hypertrophy research.

Meso Gym Pro subscription

The Single plan comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers through the store; after that, or without a subscription, a subscription is needed to plan and log workouts (your existing plans, history and exports stay available). Single costs £9.99 a month or £59.99 a year. Family costs £24.99 a month or £149.99 a year, has no free trial, and covers the organiser plus up to 4 people, on iPhone or Android. Payment is taken by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) when a Single trial ends, or when you subscribe and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your store account settings. Refunds follow the store's policy.

Accounts and Family

Single needs no account. To start or join a Family plan you need a Meso Gym account (email, Google or Apple). The organiser buys the subscription in their own store and invites members with one-use codes valid for 7 days. Members have Meso Gym Pro while the organiser's Family subscription is active; it ends if the subscription ends, if the organiser removes them, or if they leave. Only the organiser can manage or cancel the Family subscription. It is a Meso Gym feature, not Apple's family sharing. You can delete your account at any time in the Account tab.

Your data

Your workouts stay on your device, even with an account. See the privacy policy.

Third-Party Video Content

Exercise demo videos are embedded from YouTube and remain the property of their respective creators. Meso Gym does not own, host or endorse this content, and its use is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service (hosted on youtube.com, so on some phones it opens in the YouTube app). Creators can ask for a video to be removed by contacting us.

Meso Gym is a progressive training planner. Members subscribe for its programming, set tracking and training tools; demo videos are an optional reference shown alongside each exercise and are not the product. Meso Gym does not scrape or mass-aggregate YouTube content. Each video is individually selected and shown through YouTube's official embedded player, in line with YouTube's API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies, so the original creator keeps their views, attribution and control over their video.
