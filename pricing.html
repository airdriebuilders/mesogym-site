Questions

Pricing Questions

Is there a free trial?

Individual starts with a 7-day free Pro trial (one per person or payment method). Family and trainer plans start straight away with no trial.

Why is it cheaper here than in the app?

Website plans cost less than the same plan bought through the App Store or Google Play. In the UK, Individual is £9 a month here against £10 in the app. Sign in with the same Meso Gym account and the app unlocks straight away.

Monthly or yearly?

Pick either at checkout. Yearly works out cheaper than 12 months.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Use Manage Subscription on the subscribe page to change your card, see invoices or cancel. Cancelling keeps your plan until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Which currency will I pay in?

Choose GBP, USD, CAD, AUD or NZD above and checkout charges in that currency.

Do my clients or family pay anything?

No. Everyone you invite gets Meso Gym Pro on your plan.

Which plan do I need as a personal trainer?

PT Starter covers you plus 4 clients and Pro Trainer covers you plus 9. If you work with other trainers, Studio adds up to 3 more trainers with their own logins and up to 20 people in total.

Which plan do I need for my gym?

Small Gym is for up to 100 members, Large Gym is for one location with over 100 members, and Corporate is for a group with several locations. Each tier includes everything in the one before it.

Can I coach from my iPhone?

Clients and members can train on iPhone. The Trainer Dashboard is in the Android app and the web app, so coaches on an iPhone use app.mesogym.com in the browser.
