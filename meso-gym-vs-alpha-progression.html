Why Pick Meso Gym?

Pick Meso Gym if you like RP-style training: every block is a mesocycle with a deload, and next week's sets rise or fall from your pump, soreness and workload ratings. A Family plan covers up to 5 people, and Import From RP brings your RP history across.

For Trainers And Gyms: Meso Gym Trainer plans give every client Pro on their own phone, and you follow their training on the Trainer Dashboard. See Trainer Plans

FAQ

Is Meso Gym an Alpha Progression alternative?

Yes. Both are hypertrophy apps with plan building, RIR and deloads, and both are $79.99 a year in the US. Meso Gym builds every block as a mesocycle with a deload and adjusts next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload feedback; Alpha Progression focuses on weight and rep recommendations from your logged sets.

How does Meso Gym compare with Alpha Progression on price?

Both are $79.99 a year in the US. For the same price, Meso Gym builds a deload into every block and sets next week's sets from your pump, soreness and workload. Meso Gym Pro starts with a 7-day Pro trial, and Family covers up to 5 people for $199.99 a year.

Does Alpha Progression have a family plan?

We couldn't find a family plan listed on Alpha Progression's website (last checked Oct 2026). Meso Gym Family covers up to 5 people for $33.99 a month or $199.99 a year in the US.

Can I import my RP Hypertrophy data into Meso Gym?

Yes. Import From RP brings across your finished mesocycles, logged sets and unstarted plans. See Switch From RP.

Is there a Meso Gym web app?

Yes. Alongside the iPhone and Android apps, there's a web app: sign in on any browser with your Meso Gym account.

Can personal trainers and gyms monitor clients in Meso Gym?

Meso Gym Trainer plans (PT Starter, Pro Trainer, Studio and Gym) give each client full Meso Gym Pro on their own phone; clients join with an invite code. You can monitor your clients' logged workouts and progress on the Trainer Dashboard in the Android app and the web app (not the iPhone app). We couldn't find coach or client features on Alpha Progression's official site (last checked Oct 2026). See Trainer Plans.
